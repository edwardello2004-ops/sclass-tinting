About the shop

A Brampton tint bay that sweats the edges.

S Class Auto Tinting is a window tinting service on Devon Road in Brampton, installing real nano-ceramic film with 22+ years of experience behind it. It is the kind of work you notice when you look closely at the corners.

Drivers come in for privacy, for comfort on hot days, or simply because a car looks finished with the right shade on the glass. Customers mention quick turnarounds, friendly staff and fair pricing.
