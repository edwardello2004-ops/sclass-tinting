About the shop

S-Class Auto Tinting works out of a unit on Devon Road in Brampton, installing real nano-ceramic window film with more than 22 years of experience behind it. It is the kind of work you notice when you look closely at the corners.

Drivers come in for privacy, for comfort on hot days, or simply because a car looks finished with the right shade on the glass. Customers mention quick turnarounds, friendly staff and fair pricing.

Services

Glass, paint and every finish in between.

Pricing depends on the vehicle and the job. Call or book for a quote.

  1. Window tinting

    Real nano-ceramic film, cut and installed by hand. Made to tame heat and glare with a clean, non-metallic look.

    See the shade guide

  2. Paint protection filmPPF

    A clear film over high-impact paint to help guard against stone chips, scuffs and everyday road wear.

  3. Vehicle wraps

    Change the colour or finish of your car with vinyl, from satin to gloss, while the factory paint stays underneath.

  4. Chrome deleteDechroming

    Bright trim, badges and accents blacked out for a cleaner, darker silhouette that matches tinted glass.

  5. Brake caliper painting

    A bold colour behind the spokes. Calipers refinished so they stand out through the wheels.

  6. Ceramic coating

    A hard, glossy protective layer that keeps paint easier to clean and the shine deep.

Tint guide

Pick your shade.

Tint is rated by VLT, visible light transmission: the share of light the film lets through. The lower the number, the darker the glass. Tap a shade to see the view from the driver's seat.
